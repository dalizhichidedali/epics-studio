About EPICS

EPICS (Enspire Potential: Immersive Cleantech Studio) is a cutting-edge program designed to cultivate the next generation of building operators and retrofit professionals.

Through hands-on learning experiences and industry partnerships, we bridge the gap between education and real-world application in sustainable building technologies.

Our Modules

Building Operations

Learn the fundamentals of building systems, energy management, and operational excellence through practical, hands-on training.

Retrofit Technologies

Discover cutting-edge retrofit solutions for existing buildings to improve efficiency and sustainability.

CleanTech Innovation

Explore emerging clean technologies and their applications in modern building management.
